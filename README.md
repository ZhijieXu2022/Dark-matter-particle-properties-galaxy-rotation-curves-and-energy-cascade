# Dark matter particle properties from galaxy rotation curves and the theory of energy cascade

Xu Z., 2026, *Dark matter particle properties from galaxy rotation curves and the theory of energy cascade*, Zenodo [doi:10.5281/zenodo.24094453](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24094453); the earlier version is hosted on arXiv as [arXiv:2202.07240](https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.07240).

## Major findings

- Collisionless dark matter passes kinetic energy from small haloes to large ones at a scale-independent rate ε<sub>u</sub>, and the cosmic energy equation fixes the potential-to-kinetic energy ratio at 7/5 in the matter era (Virgo SCDM: ε<sub>u</sub> = −4.58×10⁻⁷ m²s⁻³ from the kinetic energy of all particles).
- In Illustris-1-Dark the all-particle estimate falls from 7.9 to 3.3×10⁻⁷ m²s⁻³ between z = 12 and z = 0 and approaches the halo-particle rate, 2.6×10⁻⁷ m²s⁻³, as the mass fraction in haloes grows to 55 per cent.
- A constant rate implies a two-thirds law for velocities and a four-thirds law, ρ<sub>r</sub> ∝ |ε<sub>u</sub>|<sup>2/3</sup> G⁻¹ r⁻⁴/³, for the mean density within r.
- The 409 rotation-curve haloes of SPARC, the DiskMass Survey and Sofue's compilation follow the four-thirds law over 3.9 decades in radius with a pooled slope of −1.37 (95 per cent interval −1.44 to −1.30), although single samples range from −1.07 to −1.77.
- If gravity is the only interaction, the cascade continues below the scale where quantum effects set in, giving m<sub>X</sub> = (256|ε<sub>u</sub>|ħ⁵/G⁴)<sup>1/9</sup> = 0.91×10¹² GeV, l<sub>X</sub> = 3.13×10⁻¹³ m and an energy scale ħ/t<sub>X</sub> = 0.87 neV.
- Because m<sub>X</sub> ∝ |ε<sub>u</sub>|<sup>1/9</sup>, the full range of rates allowed by the data moves the mass only between 0.82 and 0.99×10¹² GeV.
- The same mass follows independently from free streaming.
- A self-interaction of 1 cm² g⁻¹ dominates the cascade only below 0.046 pc.
